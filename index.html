Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 31 March 2026

Developer: Kaushal Sadhu

App: TaskFlow Daily

TaskFlow Daily ("the App") is a personal productivity application designed to help you manage tasks and reminders on your device. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

The App does NOT collect, transmit, or store personal data on external servers.

All your data (tasks, reminders, preferences) is stored locally on your device.

2. Biometric & Device Authentication

The App may use your device’s authentication methods such as fingerprint, face unlock, or PIN.

  • No biometric data is stored or accessed by the App
  • Authentication is handled entirely by your device’s operating system
  • The App only receives a success/failure response

3. Notifications & Alarms

The App uses local notifications to remind you about tasks.

  • All notifications are generated on your device
  • No data is sent to any external server
  • Alarm permissions are used for accurate scheduling

4. Device Permissions

  • Notifications – to send task reminders
  • Alarm permission – to schedule exact reminders
  • Boot permission – to restore reminders after device restart
  • Vibration – for alert feedback
  • Biometric – for secure access
  • Storage – for import/export functionality only

5. Data Storage & Security

All app data is stored locally on your device. We do not use any external servers to store your information.

6. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob Ads)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain data to show relevant ads.

Data that may be collected automatically by Google includes:

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • App interaction data (such as ad views and clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google according to their privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do NOT control how Google uses this data.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by changing your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data. However, third-party services like Google AdMob may process data as described above.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. However, ads served by Google AdMob may use general data as per their policies.

  • You can uninstall the App to remove all stored data
